Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

At FreeGeny, we are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our educational platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Parent/Guardian Information: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Child Information: First name, age range, learning preferences (no personally identifiable information)
  • School/Institution Information: Organization name, contact details, license information

1.2 Usage Data

  • Learning progress and performance metrics
  • Activity logs and session duration
  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address (for security purposes only)

1.3 Third-Party Authentication

When you sign in using Google, Facebook, Apple, Microsoft, or LinkedIn, we receive:

  • Your name and email address
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Provider ID (for account linking)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Educational Purposes: Personalize learning experiences and track progress
  • Account Management: Authenticate users and manage subscriptions
  • Communication: Send important updates, progress reports, and service notifications
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance our platform
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FreeGeny is designed for children ages 3-12. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information from children without verifiable parental consent
  • Parents have full control over their child's account and data
  • We do not use children's data for targeted advertising
  • All child data is encrypted and securely stored
  • Parents can review, delete, or request their child's data at any time by contacting us at privacy@freegeny.com

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share data only in these limited cases:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication and database hosting
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • School Administrators: If your child's account is managed by a school, authorized educators may access learning data

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Encrypted password storage
  • Regular security audits
  • Restricted access to personal data
  • Secure cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud)

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for:

  • Authentication and session management
  • Remembering user preferences
  • Security and fraud prevention

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export: Download your child's learning data
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@freegeny.com

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained as long as the account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Legal obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in servers located in the United States and European Union. We ensure all transfers comply with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, COPPA).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or prominent notice on our platform. Continued use of FreeGeny after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@freegeny.com
  • Website: www.freegeny.com
