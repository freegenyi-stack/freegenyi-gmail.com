1. Introduction and Scope

1.1. Welcome to FreeGeny Academy

FreeGeny Academy ("FreeGeny," "we," "us," or "our") operates a global digital learning ecosystem. Our Services include: our primary website at https://freegeny.com; our mobile applications ("FreeGeny App") available on iOS, Android, and Huawei AppGallery; our web-based learning platform; educator portals; administrative dashboards; microsites; landing pages; APIs; SDKs; and all associated services, tools, content, and functionalities (collectively, the "Services").

1.2. Entity Designation and Roles

FreeGeny operates through the following entities, which act as joint data controllers for the purposes specified herein:

  • FreeGeny Inc. (Delaware, USA) - Global headquarters and primary data controller
  • FreeGeny Academy SARL (Paris, France) - EU establishment and GDPR representative
  • FreeGeny Academy FZ-LLC (Dubai, UAE) - MENA region controller
  • FreeGeny Academy Consulting Co., Ltd. (Shanghai, China) - APAC region controller

1.3. Our Binding Commitment to Privacy

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") constitutes a legally binding contract between you and FreeGeny. It exhaustively details our practices concerning the collection, use, disclosure, processing, storage, transfer, anonymization, aggregation, and protection of personal information for all user categories:

  • Parents/Legal Guardians ("Parents"): Adults (18+) who register, manage accounts, provide consent, and oversee the educational journey.
  • Learners/Minors ("Children"): Individuals under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction (typically under 13-16 years) for whom accounts are created.
  • Educators & Content Creators: Independent contractors, teachers, and institutional partners who create and deliver educational content.
  • School & Institutional Administrators: Representatives of educational institutions using our platform.
  • General Visitors & Prospects: Individuals browsing our marketing properties.

1.4. Express Consent and Agreement

By accessing, downloading, installing, registering for, or using any FreeGeny Services, you explicitly:

  • Acknowledge you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Policy in its entirety.
  • If you are a Parent, you provide verifiable parental consent for the collection and use of your child's personal information as described herein.
  • Consent to international data transfers as outlined in Section 9.
  • Agree to electronic communication for all policy-related notices.

If you do not agree with ANY provision of this Policy, you must immediately discontinue all use of Services and not create an account.

2. Detailed Information We Collect

2.A. Information Provided Directly by You

2.A.1. Account Registration & Profile Creation

Parent Profile (Mandatory):

  • Primary Contact: Valid, verifiable email address (e.g., parent@domain.com).
  • Authentication Credentials: Self-selected password (hashed via bcrypt with per-user salt).
  • Account Metadata: Account creation timestamp, IP address at registration, referral source.
  • Communication Preferences: Opt-in status for marketing emails, push notification settings, language, timezone.

Child/Learner Profile (Created & Controlled by Parent):

  • Pseudonymous Identifier: First name or nickname (optional, 1-25 characters).
  • Developmental Data: Age range (e.g., 3-5, 6-8) or birth month/year (optional).
  • Interest Profile: Up to 5 selected interest tags from our curated list (e.g., #astronomy, #marine-biology, #robotics).
  • Digital Representation: Selection of 1 of 50+ non-identifiable, culturally neutral avatars.
  • Accessibility Settings: Preferred text size, color contrast mode, audio/visual preference flags.

2.A.2. Financial Transactions & Payment Processing

Payment Orchestration:

  • All payment processing is delegated to PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors: Stripe, Apple, Google, PayPal.
  • We NEVER collect, process, or store: Full credit/debit card numbers, CVV/CVC codes, bank account numbers, or persistent payment tokens on our infrastructure.

Payment Webhook Data (Received post-transaction):

  • Transaction UUID, timestamp, amount, currency, status.
  • Payment method type, last 4 digits (for disputes), billing info.
  • Subscription details and refund logs.

2.A.3. User-Generated Content & Learning Artifacts

Child-Created Content: Textual Responses, Digital Artwork, Project Submissions, Audio Recordings (encrypted, max 30s).

Progress Telemetry: Completion status, time spent, scores, badges, error patterns.

Communication Records: Parent-to-Support transcripts, Child-to-System responses.

2.A.4. Optional Sensory Data & Permissions

  • Microphone Access: Real-time stream → Google Speech-to-Text → Text response → Audio deletion (raw audio never hits disk).
  • Camera Access: AR Learning (device-only), Artifact Capture (encrypted upload, deleted after 365 days).
  • Photo Library: Single-image selection for avatar.

2.A.5. Promotional Activities & Referral Programs

Refer-a-Friend (hashed emails), Sweepstakes (winner publication), Tax Documentation (W-9 for prizes > $600).

2.A.6. Research & Feedback Participation

Beta Testing, Academic Research (IRB certified), Voluntary Surveys (NPS).

2.B. Information Collected Automatically

2.B.1. Technical & Device Fingerprint

Device Identifiers (IDFA, UUID), Hardware Specs, OS/Browser Telemetry, Network Characteristics (Anonymized IP).

2.B.2. Behavioral & Usage Telemetry

Session Analytics (heatmaps, scroll depth, video play), Performance Metrics (crash reports, latency), Event Tracking.
