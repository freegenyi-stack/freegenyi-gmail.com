Data Deletion Instructions

At FreeGeny, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your data. According to Facebook (Meta) Platform rules, we provide this page to help users delete their data associated with our application.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, including information retrieved via Facebook Login, please follow the steps below.

How to request data deletion:

  1. Send an email to privacy@freegeny.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".
  3. Provide the email address associated with your FreeGeny account.

Alternative: Remove FreeGeny from your Facebook Account

If you signed up using Facebook, you can also revoke access directly through Facebook:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Look for Apps and Websites.
  3. Find FreeGeny and click Remove.

What happens next?

Upon receiving your request via email, our team will verify your identity. Once confirmed, all your personal data, child progress data, and account information will be permanently deleted from our servers within 14 business days.

For any further questions, please visit our Privacy Policy.
